How it works

Light is the difference between a thriving plant and a struggling one.

House Plant Assistant works out how much light each spot in your home really gets, then matches plants to it. Three steps and you're set.

  1. 1

    Map your windows

    Add each window and say which way it faces. Choose a direction yourself, or lay your phone on the sill and let the compass do it. Snap a photo so you remember the spot.

  2. 2

    See the light through the year

    Using your latitude and the time of year, the app shows how bright each spot is in winter, spring, summer and autumn, and how that changes as you move back from the window.

  3. 3

    Match plants to spots

    Get plants that suit each window and how far back to sit them, with a heads-up when direct sun could scorch the leaves of a sensitive plant.

Your windows

Every window in your home, mapped.

Build a picture of the natural light in your home, one window at a time.

  • Pick a direction, or use your phone's compass at the window
  • Say how clear the view is: open sky, a sheer curtain, or a big tree outside
  • Add photos of the sill or space, so you can picture it later

Light through the seasons

The sun moves. Your plants' light does too.

A south window that bakes a cactus in winter can be gentle in summer. The app follows the sun so your suggestions do too.

  • See winter, spring, summer and autumn side by side
  • Choose how far from the window the plant will sit
  • Works in both hemispheres, set automatically from your location
  • Gets a warning when direct sun could scorch a sensitive plant

Your collection

A gallery for the plants you love.

Keep a photo of every plant, note where it sits, and see at a glance whether it's getting the light it likes.

  • A photo gallery of your whole collection
  • A quick verdict for each plant: great fit, will cope, too bright or too dim
  • One tap to move a plant to a better spot
  • Show off mode: swipe full-screen through your plants for a friend or family member

At the nursery

Know where it will go before you buy it.

Standing in front of a plant you can't resist? Find out which of your windows suit it, and how far back to sit it.

  • Search the built-in plant guide, or pick the light level printed on the plant's tag, so it works for any plant
  • See your windows ranked, with how the plant will cope through the year
  • Looking for a plant for a bare sill? Pick the spot and see what fits, hiding the plants you already own
